Maurten Fueling Protocol

Hourly carbohydrate & electrolyte strategy targeting 80–90g carbs/hr on bike and 60–75g carbs/hr on run

Pre-Race • Hotel & T-60m

Morning Priming

  • Hotel (03:30 AM): Light oatmeal/toast, water, and 1x Solid Fuel 160.
  • Drive (04:15 AM): Sip 500ml Drink Mix 160 / 320 slowly in car.
  • Canal (06:15 AM): 1x Gel 100 / Gel 100 Caf 100 with water 15 mins before start.
Total: ~105g Carbs
Bike • ~5h 48m

80–90g Carbs / Hr

  • Bottles: 2x 750ml bottles with Drink Mix 320 (80g carbs each).
  • Special Needs: Restock 1x Drink Mix 320 at 90 km turnaround.
  • Gels: 1x Gel 100 every 45 mins. Use Gel 100 Caf 100 before Kalvarienberg & Solar Hill.
  • Water: Plain water bottle on BTA mount to rinse palate.
Bike Total: ~480g Carbs
Run • ~4h 34m

60–75g Carbs / Hr

  • Gel Schedule: 1x Gel 100 every 25–30 mins (~5 km).
  • Caffeine Dosing: Gel 100 Caf 100 at km 15, km 25, and km 35 (Büchenbach climb).
  • Aid Stations: Walk aid stations, sip 150ml pure water with every gel.
Run Total: ~280g Carbs
Post-Race Finish

Recovery Window

  • Immediate (0–30m): Maurten Drink Mix 160 or recovery electrolyte blend.
  • Hydration: 500–750ml water per hour with sodium replenishment.
  • Meal (1–2h): Balanced post-race meal in athlete garden.

Tactical Execution

Discipline pacing and segment rules

Cycling Strategy

Bike: 31 km/h Target (~5h 48m)

  • Solarer Berg: Maintain high cadence (85+ RPM) in an easy gear. Avoid surging on crowd excitement.
  • Kalvarienberg (Greding): Cap power below functional threshold to preserve running legs.
  • Aero Positioning: Stay locked in aero on flat sectors to sustain the 31 km/h speed target.
  • Nutrition Timing: Sip Drink Mix 320 every 15–20 minutes to keep gastric emptying optimal.
Running Strategy

Run: 6:30/km Target (~4h 34m)

  • Canal Towpath: Hold an exact 6:25–6:30/km pace along the flat first 25 km.
  • Aid Stations: Walk 20–30 seconds at each station for complete fluid and electrolyte intake.
  • Büchenbach: Manage effort on the 35km incline before the descent into the stadium.
  • Maurten Routine: Take 1x Gel 100 with plain water; avoid mixing on-course sports drink.
